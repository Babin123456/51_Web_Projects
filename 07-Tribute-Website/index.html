Missile Man & People's President

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he studied physics and aerospace engineering before spending four decades as a scientist and administrator, predominantly at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). Kalam led India's civilian space program and missile development efforts, playing key roles in the SLV-III launch and the Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
